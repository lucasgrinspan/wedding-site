Dress Code Formal

Suits (maybe a tie), and floor-length or formal midi dresses. It can get chilly once the sun goes down, so bring a layer.

Our wedding palette features dark greens and warm browns (just in case you want to match).

The ceremony is outdoors on the lawn (you might want to skip the stilettos), and cocktail hour is on a stone courtyard.

It's an adult-only night, so keep the little ones at home.
